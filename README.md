# WHO-Comparison-PowerBI
Use of Power BI in order to compare the World Health Organization data on selected indicators
* GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$) or NY.GDP.PCAP.KD
 This represents the economic capacity, as it shows the total income earned throughout the year.
* School Enrollment for Primary and Secondary Education or SE.PRM.ENRR and
SE.SEC.ENRR
 This provides an insight into the education access for the people.
* Under-5 Mortality Rate or SH.DYN.MORT
 Gives an idea of the health of the public
* Current health expenditure per capita or SH.XPD.CHEX.PC.CD
 Provides the amount invested for health
* Life Expectancy at birth , total (years) or SP.DYN.LE00.IN
 Shows how the conditions in the countries or region have affected mortality
